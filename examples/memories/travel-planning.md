# Travel Planning

## Documents
- Passport expiry checked at booking time, not at check-in. Renewal takes
  longer than expected every single time.
- Keep scans of passport, insurance, and tickets in a single encrypted folder.

## Booking rules
- Direct flights when the price gap is under €100; connections otherwise.
- Hotels: walkability beats star rating. Read the last 20 reviews, not the
  score.

## Packing
- One carry-on for anything under a week. The packing list lives here rather
  than in the suitcase.
- Chargers and adapters in the top pocket — the only items genuinely missed
  when forgotten.
