# Money Admin

## Recurring
- Tax filing deadline: 30 April. Documents collected through March.
- Quarterly estimated payments; the last one always sneaks up.

## Rules that saved money
- Deduct: home office share, hardware over a set threshold, professional
  memberships, and mileage on client visits. Receipts live in a dated folder,
  not in an inbox.
- Keep every service receipt for anything insured — see `car-log.md` for the
  tire-change discount filing.

## Notes
- One account for bills, one for everything else. Mixing them is how budgets
  quietly fail.
- Annual review of subscriptions each January; cancel anything unused twice in
  a row.
